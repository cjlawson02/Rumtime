## Mounting

- No panel flange. The female plug sits in a printed holder set into the drawer face, socket flush with the outside. The holder clamps the body so pulling the PSU plug doesn't load the screw terminals.
- Builder choice (2026-11-01): use it as the PSU intake despite the 2 A listing. It must pass the heat test in Test 11.7 ([`14-bench-test-protocol.md`](../../../docs/14-bench-test-protocol.md)). If it fails, cut the PSU plug and hardwire the lead into J1 through a strain relief.
